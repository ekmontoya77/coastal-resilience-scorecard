User Groups

Built for the full ecosystem

Coastal resilience is not the responsibility of any single actor. The Scorecard is designed as common ground — a shared diagnostic that lets each group see its own exposure, contribution, and route to action within the same evidence base.

🏛️

City & Government Teams

Municipal resilience offices, urban planners, coastal zone managers, and public infrastructure agencies undertaking multi-generational coastal strategy development.

🏢

Private Sector & Investors

Asset managers, real estate developers, infrastructure operators, insurers, and blue finance investors with material exposure to coastal zones and climate risk.

🌱

Startups & Innovators

Nature-tech ventures, coastal monitoring platforms, climate insurtech companies, and risk analytics firms solving for coastal resilience at scale.

🤝

Communities & NGOs

Coastal communities, grassroots resilience organizations, and civil society groups working to protect livelihoods, housing, and natural assets in vulnerable zones.

🔬

Research & Academia

Coastal scientists, climate modellers, urban resilience researchers, and applied institutions generating the evidence base for adaptive strategy and policy.

🌐

Multilateral & DFIs

Development finance institutions, UN agencies, climate funds, and multilateral development banks deploying capital and technical assistance for coastal resilience.

Each group reaches capital differently. See Finance for the routes available by project context and ownership structure — or join the Scorecard to get started.
